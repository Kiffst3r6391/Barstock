## Prestonfield Golf Club: taking control of the bar with BarStock

### About the club

Prestonfield Golf Club is a member-run golf club in Edinburgh. The bar is a key revenue stream, but stock control was historically manual: weekly stocktakes on paper, EPOS reports reviewed separately, and gross profit worked out in spreadsheets at month end.

### The challenge

Before BarStock, the club paid an external stocktaker **£140 every six weeks**. That was over **£1,200 a year** just to be told what stock was missing — and the results only arrived after the count, with no live view of stock levels or GP between visits.

The club also recognised the same issues many clubs have:

- Stocktakes were a manual, time-consuming process with inconsistent results.
- Gross profit was only calculated at month end, so underperforming lines went unnoticed for weeks.
- Popular draught lines occasionally ran out during busy periods because current stock was not visible in real time.
- Retiring a product meant losing its sales history.
- Sales from the EPOS had to be reconciled against stock by hand.

### The solution

BarStock replaced the external stocktake service and brought stock control in-house. The full product list was loaded, EPOS product names were mapped to stock items, and cost and sell prices were set for every line. Staff were shown how to:

1. Upload the weekly EPOS sales CSV.
2. Record deliveries, wastage and supplier returns.
3. Run a stocktake and review the variance report.
4. Generate the order sheet for the next supplier run.

The system also gave the club a single look — chalk paper, bottle green and copper, with serif figures and ruled tables — used on screen and in the printed committee pack.
