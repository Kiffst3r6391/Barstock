Hi [First Name],

I am Chris from Prestonfield Golf Club. Over the past year I have been using and refining a bar stock management system that has made a real difference to how we run our bar, and I am now making it available to other clubs.

The system is called **BarStock** and it is designed specifically for member clubs and small venues that do not have an enterprise EPOS or warehouse system, but still need proper stock control and GP visibility.

**What it replaces**

If your club is anything like ours was, the bar is run on a mix of EPOS reports, paper stock sheets and spreadsheets. Stocktakes take hours, GP is only known at month end, and it is easy to run out of a popular line on a busy night without warning.

Before BarStock, we paid an external stocktaker £140 every six weeks. That was over £1,200 a year just to find out what stock was missing. BarStock replaces that entirely: stocktakes are done in-house, sales deductions are automatic, and the committee can see GP and variance any time.

BarStock brings it all into one place:

- **Stocktakes** — full or partial counts with automatic variance reports showing counted vs expected stock, valued at cost, plus a running surplus or shortfall across counts.
- **Sales upload** — upload the weekly EPOS CSV and stock deducts automatically. Half pints, shandies and other child products are handled through configurable parent-stock mappings.
- **Live reports** — dashboard summary plus sales analytics, stock variance, division and comprehensive product-level GP reports.
- **Ordering** — printable order sheets grouped by supplier and product type.
- **Invoice pricing** — check supplier invoice prices against expected costs and update in bulk, with price locking where needed.
- **Backups** — automatic daily backups plus pre-operation snapshots and one-click restore.

**How it is licensed**

We keep the pricing simple, and stock setup is included with both options:

- **£1,000 one-time purchase** — full app, no ongoing fees, initial stock setup included.
- **£750 per year lease with support** — the app, all updates, email support and initial stock setup.

There is no cloud subscription, no user limit and no internet required after setup. All data stays on your own Mac.

BarStock is a macOS app, so it will fit clubs that already use Macs for admin or bar management. The interface is deliberately quiet — chalk paper, bottle green and copper, serif figures and ruled tables — and the reports print in the same style, which matters when the pack goes to committee.
