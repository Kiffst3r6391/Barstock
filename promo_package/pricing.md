## What the purchase includes

- All current features

## What the lease includes

Everything in the purchase, plus:

- All updates during the year
- Email support
- **Initial stock setup and product import included**

There is **no cloud subscription**, **no user limit** and **no internet required** after setup. All data stays on the club's Mac.

## How to position this with clubs

- **Lead with the external stocktake.** At Prestonfield that was £140 every six weeks — over £1,200 a year — for a report that arrived after the count. Either licence is cheaper than that.
- **Lease is the easiest sell.** £750 a year is less than one busy night's takings for most club bars, and it includes support.
- **Purchase suits committees that prefer capital spend.** £1,000 once, no recurring commitment.
- **Offer a 30-day trial.** Letting a club use it with their own product list removes the risk.
- **Show the print.** Committees read paper. Reports print in the same chalk-and-bottle-green style as the screen, so the pack looks considered before a number is read.

## Compared with an external stocktaker

At Prestonfield the club paid an external stocktaker £140 every six weeks — over £1,200 a year — just to be told what stock was missing. BarStock replaces that cost entirely and gives the committee live stock and GP numbers all year round.

- **Purchase at £1,000:** pays for itself in under one year versus an external stocktaker.
- **Lease at £750/year:** still cheaper than the external stocktaker, and includes support and updates.
